2. Standard Bars (Section 2.3.1)
Trading occurs when information arrives, not when the physical clock ticks. Standard bars group raw transaction data:
Bar Type
Sampling Rule
Statistical Properties & AFML Verdict
Time Bars (1m, 5m)
Sampled every fixed time interval T (seconds/minutes).
<b>Flawed:</b> High heteroscedasticity, multi-modal variance, and strong autocorrelation.
Tick Bars (T500, T1000)
Sampled every fixed number of transactions N (Mandelbrot & Taylor 1967).
<b>Good Proxy:</b> Synchronizes with market activity speed. Ideal for Forex without volume.

The Two Families of Information Bars:
 1. Imbalance Bars (TIB, VIB, DIB): Sampled when cumulative signed order flow departures exceed expectation. Theta_T =
 Sum(b_t * v_t). Trigger condition: |Theta_T| >= E_0[T] * |2*P[b_t=1] - 1|.
2. Runs Bars (TRB, VRB, DRB): Sampled when unbroken runs of one-sided volume exceed expectation. Captures aggressive
 institutional sweeps across the order book.
4. Multi-Product Series & The ETF Trick (Section 2.4)
Futures contracts expire periodically, causing artificial price gaps on roll dates. The ETF Trick synthesizes a continuous,
unleveraged, reinvested index tracking a basket of instruments without artificial price drift.
Properties of the ETF Trick:
 • Avoids negative prices produced by traditional backward difference stitching.
 • Correctly accounts for contract multipliers, financing costs, and roll transaction friction.
 • Handles multi-asset spreads (e.g., S&P; 500 / Eurostoxx 50) using PCA eigen-weights.
 
5. Feature Downsampling & The CUSUM Filter (Section 2.5)
A structured dataset of millions of bars contains mostly random noise. The Symmetric CUSUM Filter is a
quality-control mechanism designed to capture only significant shifts in the return process (catalytic events).
Symmetric CUSUM Filter Equations:
   Upside Accumulator:   S+_t = max(0, S+_{t-1} + y_t - E_{t-1}[y_t]),   S+_0 = 0
   Downside Accumulator: S-_t = min(0, S-_{t-1} + y_t - E_{t-1}[y_t]),   S-_0 = 0
   Event Trigger Condition:   S_t = max(S+_t, -S-_t) >= h
   Directional Reset: When S+_t >= h, record event and reset S+_t = 0 (same for S-_t).
Why CUSUM Outperforms Bollinger Bands:
 When price hovers around a Bollinger band, it produces dozens of repeated false breakout alarms. The CUSUM filter resets
 to zero immediately upon breach, requiring a completely fresh move of size h to trigger again.

7. Chapter 2 Exercises Key Takeaways
Exercise
Core Question
Takeaway & Conclusion
Ex 2.1
Tick vs Volume vs Dollar bars statistical comparison
Dollar bars win: lowest serial correlation, smallest variance of variances, closest to Normal IID.
Ex 2.2
Dollar bars vs Dollar Imbalance Bars (DIB)
DIB exhibits higher serial correlation because it concentrates on one-sided institutional order flow.
Ex 2.3
S&P 500 / Eurostoxx 50 spread via ETF Trick
PCA weights construct a stationary, cointegrated portfolio spread confirmed via ADF test (p < 0.05).
Ex 2.4
Bollinger bands vs CUSUM filter (h = 0.05)
CUSUM is vastly less heteroscedastic; zero-floor reset eliminates false repetitive hovering triggers.
Ex 2.5
CUSUM on returns vs absolute returns |y_t|
Absolute return sampling captures pure volatility expansions, delivering the most homoscedastic sample.
